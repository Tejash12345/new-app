3D character models in this folder are by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com),
released under CC0 (Public Domain) — free for commercial use, no attribution
required. Mirrored via Poly Pizza (https://poly.pizza).

Animals (Animated Animal Pack): wolf, fox, shiba, husky, deer, stag, bull, horse
Humans/characters: runner (Animated Human), robot (Animated Robot),
  hero (Animated Platformer Character), adventurer (Adventurer), astronaut (Astronaut)
Vehicles (Cars Bundle, via poly.pizza): car, pickup, truck (armored), police,
  taxi, suv, sportscar
  — motorbike, aeroplane and helicopter are procedural (built in code).

The hero Lion is built from wolf.glb (gold + a procedural mane).
lion.glb is an OPTIONAL drop-in slot for a realistic lion — not shipped by default.
